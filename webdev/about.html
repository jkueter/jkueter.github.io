A quick intro to Dr. Kueter

Come join me on a journey as I share my passion for teaching, learning, technology and mathematics.

I am the definition of a life-long learner, motivated teacher, and passionate leader with a desire to make education better for students and teachers. My first, and probably last, educational role is as a learner. Even before entering school, I was always wanting to learn. This probably came from beign the youngest of three children, but I was always cracing new knowlege. At a young age, I knew teaching would be my calling. There was this innate desire to help others to be successfull. I was a primary (K-2) for 28 years in Anchorage, Alaska. I loved helping students to gain confidence, learn new skills and believe they can do more than they think they can. During my teaching years, I longed to learn more. I earn a Masters and Educational Specialist degrees in Instructional Technology. A desire to learn more led me to a Doctorate of Education in STEM education. While I have retired from full time teaching, I still want to be involoved and make a difference in education.
