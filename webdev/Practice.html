It's Not New Math

Think back to your elementary school years. What was your math instruction like? Was there actual instruction? Or were you presented with a fact or an equation, told to memorize it, practiced using it on worksheets, and then took a test to see if you could use it? And then, if you were unlucky, they threw in a couple of random story problems to try and trick you. But, if you knew the equation and you put the numbers in the right place (that was the hard part), you got it right. You celebrated and moved on. If you did not get it right, oh well- it was time to move on to the next memorization anyway. How many of you experienced that in your elementary years? I remember being so good at math that the teacher just let me move on. I did worksheet after worksheet on my own and then took the test and moved to the next one. I was very good at memorizing and lucky with story problems.
